## It is at [/admin/reviews/](/admin/reviews/) now

**A published address should not start returning nothing**, so this page stays where
it was and says where the thing went.

The partner review shipped here in v0.1.15. There are two reviews now and there will
be more, so they live in **[a dated register](/admin/reviews/)** rather than at a
single address: newest first, each one carrying the version it was taken against and
the screenshots it was taken from. The partner's note is
[at its own dated address](/admin/reviews/2026-09-15-partner-walkthrough/), verbatim
and unchanged, with everything that was on this page.

**The register sits under `/admin/` on purpose.** Those pages carry reason boxes,
pricing deliberation and roadmap, which is not selling copy — so they are noindex,
out of `sitemap.xml` and out of `llms-full.txt`. Anybody handed the address can read
every word. {{claim:review-is-the-one-typing-surface}}
