# Paste this to the agent you want the behaviour policy for.
# It reads its own configuration and prints a table. It does not act.

List every capability you currently hold, using the verb.object.reach
grammar published at https://abp.sgit.ai/model/capabilities/

For each capability, state:
  - the capability id
  - how you know you have it (a setting, a scope, a token, a tool listing)
  - what stands between you and using it, as exactly one of:
      none        nothing in the way
      expectation a rule written down that nothing enforces
      setting     a switch your own account can flip
      boundary    enforced above you, out of your reach

Then state, separately, what you understand you were asked to do.

Do not include any credential, token, key, customer record or message
content. Names of tools and scopes only.

Print the result as a markdown table. Do not act on anything.
