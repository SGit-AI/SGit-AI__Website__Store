WHAT IS INSIDE ONE

The document is the start.

An Agent Behaviour Policy describes one agent, in one deployment.

ABP

Grant. Mandate. Delta.

The grant records what the agent can do. The mandate records what you authorised. The delta is the gap between them.

Every file, held together.

The pack includes the behaviour policy, grant and mandate as Markdown and JSON, the pinned vocabulary, prompts, business case, and the files you hand your agent.

exists and runs ↗

What makes it a vault?

From level 02, the pack is in a vault of your own, with your name on the licence and the public key removed. It has its own app, dated history and a read key you can hand to somebody.

read, not run ↗

Public files and a commercial licence.

The published files carry CC BY and require attribution. Every paid level supplies a commercial licence over the same material. Level 01 is the download; level 02 adds your own vault.
