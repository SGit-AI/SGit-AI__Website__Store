What the market looks like here

read, not run The market anchors between eight and one hundred and fifty thousand dollars, and nothing sits underneath it. That gap is the whole opportunity: somebody who wants to know what they granted an agent has a choice today between a five-figure engagement and nothing.

What you are actually buying

The delta: the union of what a credential permits, against what its holder is authorised and expected to do, and the excess authority between the two. It is computed when you open it and never stored. It is not a thing we keep about you.
