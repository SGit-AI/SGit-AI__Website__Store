You are backing a company, and want somebody to look

An investor who needs a review of a company they are backing, and needs it to be somebody's work rather than a scan. You arrive asking: “What would somebody who knows what they are doing find?”

What the market looks like here

read, not run No productised, affordable, signed investor review exists at any price — and every component of one is already written. It is the emptiest quadrant on this site, and the two upper tiers point straight at it.

What you are actually buying

A person's time, and in tier 4 a team's. A time confirmed, what they read before the call, and what you get afterwards in writing.

Start at tier 3 — £150 to £1,000 unless you already know you need more than it. Every card below says what arrives and what does not before it says how to pay.
