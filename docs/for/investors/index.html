You are backing a company, and want somebody to look

An investor who needs a review of a company they are backing, and needs it to be somebody's work rather than a scan. You arrive asking: “What would somebody who knows what they are doing find?”

What the market looks like here

read, not run No productised, affordable, signed investor review exists at any price — and every component of one is already written. It is the emptiest quadrant on this site, and the two upper tiers point straight at it.

What you are actually buying

A person's time, and in tier 4 a team's. A time confirmed, what they read before the call, and what you get afterwards in writing.

Start at tier 3 — £500 unless you already know you need more than it. Every card below says what arrives and what does not before it says how to pay.

No analytics. No cookies. No third-party fonts, scripts or CDN — every byte of this site is served from this domain. Every page that sells anything opens no network connection at all, and a build check holds that line. The one exception is named and narrow: a review of a vault embeds that vault from one host, through one vendored component, and says so on itself. Nothing anywhere here sends anything about you — no fetch, no beacon, no socket, on any page. Paying happens on the payment provider’s own pages, which is the only place a card number should ever be typed: nothing on this site collects one. Prices are in pounds — pricing in euros while settling in pounds adds about two per cent.
