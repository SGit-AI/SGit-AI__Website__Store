- `ABP-GDR-C` — £500 — Corrected for your situation: You tell us the industry, the use case and the details. The mandate is corrected against them and the vault comes back yours.
- `ABP-GDR-S` — £1,500 — Two sessions, and a professional signs it: Half an hour with your team to find out what is actually running, and half an hour to deliver it — reviewed and signed off by a security professional.
