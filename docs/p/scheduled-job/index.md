# A scheduled job

A job that outlives the person who made it, running as a service account nobody logs in as. Seven capabilities, four wanted, four with nothing in the way.

- It can do: 7
- You wanted: 4
- Not wanted: 4
- Nothing in the way: 4

Counts, not a score.

Free public template: https://riskmandate.ai/abp-vault-scheduled-job.html

## The four levels

- `ABP-SJB-P` — £5 — The pack, downloaded: Every file in the vault, downloaded by you. Yours to use, keep and hand to your agent.
- `ABP-SJB-V` — £50 — A working vault: The same material as a vault you hold the keys to: clone it, change it, version it, and hand anyone a read key.
- `ABP-SJB-C` — £500 — Corrected for your situation: You tell us the industry, the use case and the details. The mandate is corrected against them and the vault comes back yours.
- `ABP-SJB-S` — £1,500 — Two sessions, and a professional signs it: Half an hour with your team to find out what is actually running, and half an hour to deliver it — reviewed and signed off by a security professional.
