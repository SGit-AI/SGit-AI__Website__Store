## What arrives


### ABP Pack

Every file in the vault for the shape you picked, downloaded by you — the behaviour policy, the grant and the mandate as markdown and as JSON, the pinned vocabulary, the prompts, the business case, and the two files you hand the agent — under a commercial licence to you, rather than under the attribution licence the same files carry in public.

Included: The behaviour policy, the grant, the mandate and the delta; The agent instructions and the mapping prompt; Every file from the published vault, downloaded; A commercial licence to you over material that is public under CC BY
Outside this level: A vault you hold the keys to; Version history, or a read key you can hand on; Any correction for your deployment

### ABP Vault

A vault of your own with the pack in it, a licence carrying your name, and no public key on it.

Included: Everything in ABP Pack; A working vault with its own reading app; Your own keys, and your own version history; A read key you can hand to anybody who asks how you govern the agent
Outside this level: Correction done for you; Time with a security professional

### ABP Tailored

The vault, with the mandate corrected against your situation rather than against a starting assumption, and a written note of what changed.

Included: Everything in ABP Vault; The mandate corrected against your industry, use case and deployment; The work done and signed off by a named security professional
Outside this level: The two sessions that come with ABP Reviewed; Any guarantee about a particular outcome

### ABP Reviewed

Two half-hour sessions with your team, the behaviour policy built from the interview, and a security professional's review and sign-off.

Included: Everything in ABP Tailored; Two half-hour sessions with the professional doing the work; A custom vault, reviewed and signed off
Outside this level: Ongoing monitoring, or anything recurring; Any guarantee about a particular outcome

## Nothing can be bought here

No payment link has been issued on any level, so every buying action on this page is a disabled control carrying its own reason. There is no form, input or field anywhere in this site's output and a build check holds that line.
