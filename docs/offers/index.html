Why each number is that number

No price on this site is positioning, and none of them is the builder's. The four were set by the project lead on 15 September 2026, replacing the four tiers of the 10 September pack, and the frozen table in the build gate was re-pointed at them in the commit that says so. The check did not loosen; it was aimed somewhere else.

The numberWhy it is that number
£10 — Can I just have the pack?The floor, and it has moved twice. £10 on 10 September, because the fixed card fee is about four per cent of a fiver; £5 on 15 September by ruling anyway; £10 again on 16 September — and this time the number is not what changed. Two reviewers said £5 read as a commodity price for something sophisticated, and the answer to that is not a bigger number for the same thing. What you buy at this level is now a commercial licence over material that is public under CC BY, which is a different product rather than a dearer one
£50 — Can I have a vault I hold the keys to?The same material as the level below, as the thing itself rather than a copy of its contents. A vault is versioned, has its own app and can be handed to somebody with a read key, and none of that survives an email
£500 — Can you correct it for my situation?You send the industry, the use case and the details; the mandate is corrected against them and the delta recomputed. It is the first level where somebody other than you does the work that makes the document yours
£1,500 — Can somebody go through it with my team?Two half-hour sessions — one to interview the team, one to deliver — plus the work between them and a security professional's review. It is the only level whose cost is somebody's time, and it is a fixed price rather than a band because two sessions is what is sold
By depth band — Can I have the same result in a form the board will read?Priced against the depth band of the offer it attaches to, because the work is the rendering and the rendering scales with what there is to render
By depth band — Will somebody put their name to it?Priced against the depth band of the offer it attaches to

The floor moved, and the arithmetic that argued against it is still here. At £5 the fixed card fee alone is about four per cent of the transaction, which on 10 September was the reason the floor was £10 rather than £5. On 15 September the floor was set at £5 anyway, and on 16 September it went back to £10 — this time because of what the level carries rather than what it costs to take the money. Every step stays on the page, because a store that deletes the argument it lost is a store you cannot check. projected 10 Sep 2026

The cheapest level is a licence, not a download. The files it sends are published free under CC BY, and anybody using them that way has to attribute them. What £10 buys is a commercial licence to you over the same material — so it can go into client work, into a product, or into a document that goes to a regulator, without carrying our name into it. That is grantable because the copyright in the pack is ours: everything in it was written here. The wording is not drafted yet, and the ledger says so rather than the page implying otherwise. read, not run 16 Sep 2026

Prices are in pounds. Pricing in euros while settling in pounds adds about two per cent, and that two per cent buys nothing. projected 10 Sep 2026

How each one is paid for

Every level is a single price, so every level can hold a standing payment link. There is no band to fix and nothing to take a deposit against: that shape belonged to the offer line replaced on 15 September, and the ledger keeps it rather than pretending it was never there. read, not run 11 Sep 2026

No link has been created on either rail. Every card shows its code and its delivery page instead of a live button, and your order names which rail is missing. part exists 17 Sep 2026 How buying works.

The offer identifier is the stable part

A printed code redirects to /d/<id>/ and nothing else about it is load-bearing. Not the host, not the path above it, not a word of the copy.

That is deliberate, and it is the one piece of engineering this site does purely because of a disagreement. A ruling of 10 September placed a storefront under the risk product rather than on the platform domain. The instruction in hand overrode it and named this host. The ruling stands on the record, unedited, and the build treats the host as moveable: every internal link here is relative, the host appears in no sentence of copy, and the identifiers above do not change. If the site moves, it costs a DNS record and no reprinting. read, not run 10 Sep 2026
