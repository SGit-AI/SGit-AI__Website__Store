Nothing on this page can be bought. This is a prototype of a purchase flow, not a purchase flow. No brief reaches us, no payment link is behind anything here, and which of the five (if any) becomes the real one is not decided. specified, not built 11 Sep 2026

The work it configures is done by people, and the team specified for it has never run. Which parts of it could later be done without them is a decision nobody has taken. specified, never run 11 Sep 2026

What it is testingWhether starting from a guess beats starting from nothing. The corrections are the signal: what somebody removes from a prefilled estate says more than what they add to an empty one.
What is wrong with itA scenario that is nearly right gets accepted whole. The brief records which one it started from for exactly that reason, and it is still a risk.
What it producesOne JSON brief, identical across all five prototypes. The interface is the variable; the document is not.

This prototype needs JavaScript. Everything it does happens in this browser: there is no form on this site and no page here opens a network connection, so with scripting off there is nothing to fall back to except the description of what it would do.

store.sgit.ai

Every agent needs a licence to operate. This is where you buy one. Six offers, four of them with a price and a code, two of them listed and not yet buyable, grouped by which of three buyers each was built for. The paid thing is not access and it is not customisation. It is independence.

Nothing here is a compliance assessment, nothing here is a mark of conformity to any standard, and no opinion here is personal: the company issues every opinion, and the person who sells is never the person who signs. Outputs are model generated and marked as such.
