Tier 4 — Can somebody go through it with my team?

Two half-hour sessions with your team, the behaviour policy built from the interview, and a security professional's review and sign-off.

OfferTier 4 — t4
Price£1,500
When it arrives1 to 5 days, from your reply, not from your payment. Depends on load. The constraint is one person's calendar rather than a warehouse, and saying so is more use to you than a word borrowed from a shop that holds stock.
How it is paidPayment link. A link, and a printed code beside it. An online payment, so no cross-border rule applies to it.
What is true of it todaydelivered by a person Two sessions with a named security professional, who reviews the work and signs it off. The method is published and open to read — an agent's grant, its mandate and the delta between them, and eleven linked threat models presented at a conference in Barcelona.

Who does it

Dinis Cruz

Founder of the sgit.ai network, RiskMandate.ai, The Cyber Boardroom, MyFeeds.ai and VoiceDebrief.ai; former OWASP Board member and organiser of the OWASP Summits; creator of the O2 Platform.

Runs the correction at £500 and the two sessions at £1,500.

One name today, and the list is built for more — other security professionals are being approached to take some of this work. Everybody who runs a review here.

What arrives

  • Half an hour with your team to find out what is actually running.
  • Half an hour to deliver it and walk through what came back.
  • Everything in the corrected level, built from the interview rather than from a form.
  • Reviewed and signed off by a security professional, with a record of what was asked and answered.

What this is not, and will not become

  • Not a signed opinion as a formal instrument. A professional reviewing and signing off the work is not the same thing as the company issuing a signed opinion, and the wording for that does not exist yet.
  • Not an audit, an accreditation or a mark of conformity. None of those words describes this and none appears on the deliverable.
  • Not a risk score. What the risk of your deployment is depends on your assets and is separate work.
  • Not open-ended. Two sessions is what is sold; more is a separate conversation.
