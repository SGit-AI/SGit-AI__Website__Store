What arrives

  • Half an hour with your team to find out what is actually running.

  • Everything in the corrected level, built from the interview rather than from a form.
  • Reviewed and signed off by a security professional, with a record of what was asked and answered.

What this is not, and will not become

  • Not a signed opinion as a formal instrument. A professional reviewing and signing off the work is not the same thing as the company issuing a signed opinion, and the wording for that does not exist yet.
  • Not an audit, an accreditation or a mark of conformity. None of those words describes this and none appears on the deliverable.
  • Not a risk score. What the risk of your deployment is depends on your assets and is separate work.
  • Not open-ended. Two sessions is what is sold; more is a separate conversation.
