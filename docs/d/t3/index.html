What arrives

  • Everything in the vault level, with the mandate corrected against what you told us.
  • The delta recomputed against your corrected mandate, with the barrier on every row.
  • A written note of what was changed and why, so the correction is checkable rather than trusted.

What this is not, and will not become

  • Not a call. Nobody interviews you at this level: you send the details and the work is done from them.
  • Not signed by a named professional. That is the level above.
  • Not a compliance assessment and not a risk score. An Agent Behaviour Policy describes and does not judge.

What is taken, and when

On the order£100 — a fifth
On delivery£400, invoiced when the work is in your hands

This level has never run for a paying buyer, and a deposit is how that is sold honestly from a card: neither side carries the whole amount before anybody has done anything. The split belongs to the offer and not to the rail — a card tapped at the stand takes the same deposit as a link on a phone.

What you do

Nobody needs access to your environment and you send us no credentials. We send a prompt, you run it where the agent runs, and you send back what it printed. The mandate is corrected against that.

1You buy this level. Nothing is scheduled and nobody waits.
2You paste the prompt to the agent, in the environment it actually runs in. It reads its own configuration and prints a table.
3You send back what it printed. One table — no credentials, no logs, no access.
4The corrected vault comes back, with a written note of what changed and why.

The result is computed on your own machine and sent back in bands rather than as raw counts, because a connector list on its own is identifying. The prompt reads and prints; it does not act, and its last line says so. The whole of it is on the page that explains buying, and again on your own page after you pay.
