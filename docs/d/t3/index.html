What you do

Nobody needs access to your environment and you send us no credentials. You already have the prompt — MAP-A-GRANT.md ships in every template zip and in every vault, so it is in your hands before you pay as well as after. You run it where the agent runs and send back what it writes. The mandate is corrected against that.

1You buy this level. Nothing is scheduled and nobody waits.
2Within 24 hours a person follows up, and confirms where to send the files.
3You paste MAP-A-GRANT.md to the agent, in the environment it actually runs in. It reads its own configuration and prints a table.
4You send back the two files it writes — grant.json and mandate.json — and the session record, with no secret in them and your order reference on the message. No credentials, no logs, no access.
5The corrected vault comes back, with a written note of what changed and why.

Step 5 is estimated at one to three days from your reply — from your reply rather than from your payment, because until the details arrive there is nothing to correct against. It is an estimate rather than a measurement, and it is labelled as one; the constraint behind it is one person’s calendar. The 24 hours is riskmandate.ai’s commitment to follow up and is kept on their pages.

The result is computed on your own machine and sent back in bands rather than as raw counts, because a connector list on its own is identifying. The prompt reads and prints; it does not act, and its last line says so. The whole of it is on the page that explains buying, and again on your own page after you pay.
