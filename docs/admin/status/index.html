How this page is built

Every row is generated. A memo names its workstreams, a workstream holds its units, and a unit that is done carries the pages it produced and the release it went out in. A unit that claims to be done and points at nothing fails the release — because "done" with nothing to open is the one status that can be wrong without anybody noticing.

The memos themselves are kept word for word at the queue, with the reading of each one held separately from the memo. The board at /admin/work/ is the same units arranged by workstream rather than by where they came from.

An operations surface for the people building this store, public because every page here is. It is not a selling page and nothing on it is an offer. The shop is here · every claim, with its state · the reviews
