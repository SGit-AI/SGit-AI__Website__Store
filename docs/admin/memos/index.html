The four steps

  1. 1 · Capture

    The memo goes in this file verbatim, dated, the moment it arrives. Nothing is trimmed.

  2. 2 · Read

    A brief: what was asked, what it changes, what it contradicts, and what is not yet decidable.

  3. 3 · Break

    Units of work, each one small enough to be finished, each naming the memo it came from.

  4. 4 · Track

    The units go on the board at /admin/work/ and move there. The memo page shows their live state.

Why the memo is kept word for word

A memo is dictated, so it arrives with the repetitions and the self-corrections of speech in it. It is kept exactly as it arrived. The reading of it is written underneath and kept separate, which is the same rule the reviews follow: what somebody said and what we made of it are two different objects, and only one of them is allowed to be wrong.
