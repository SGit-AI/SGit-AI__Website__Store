Five readers walked the new store from the front page to the till. Four of them stopped at the same place.

The last panel ended by saying what it could not do: a panel is a weaker instrument than a journey, and nobody arrives at a store having read four versions of its front page. This is the journey. The same five readers, written on 15 September and carried across unchanged, each walked the store the way a buyer would, at 1280 and at 390. Four of the five reached the checkout and stopped at the same place, for the same reason, and it is not a design problem.

The two earlier runs are published as encrypted vaults with their read keys on their own pages: the personas, the protocol and every step as a record. This one is not, yet.
