Product pages that show the product

0 of 6 units done. “We go there, we don’t know what we’re buying.” Description, price and a buy button, a carousel of what you actually get, specs and SKUs, and a place for reviews. Prototyped in the lab first, reviewed, then wired in.

This workstream came out of a memo. Make a product page feel like a product page, 2026-09-16 — kept verbatim, read into a brief, and broken into the units below.
