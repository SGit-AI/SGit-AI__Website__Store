Every agent needs a licence to operate. This is where you buy one. Six offers, four of them with a price and a code, two of them listed and not yet buyable, grouped by which of three buyers each was built for. The paid thing is not access and it is not customisation. It is independence.

Nothing here is a compliance assessment, nothing here is a mark of conformity to any standard, and no opinion here is personal: the company issues every opinion, and the person who sells is never the person who signs. Outputs are model generated and marked as such.

No analytics. No cookies. No third-party fonts, scripts or CDN — every byte of this site is served from this domain. Every page that sells anything opens no network connection at all, and a build check holds that line. The one exception is named and narrow: a review of a vault embeds that vault from one host, through one vendored component, and says so on itself. Nothing anywhere here sends anything about you — no fetch, no beacon, no socket, on any page. Paying happens on the payment provider’s own pages, which is the only place a card number should ever be typed: nothing on this site collects one.
